# Branding and display rules

## Section-level attribution (required)

Show one of these near the grants list and on every grant page:

* EN: **Grant data powered by FundingPro** / **Grant opportunities provided by FundingPro**
* RU: **Данные о грантах предоставлены FundingPro**
* UZ: **Grantlar haqidagi ma’lumotlar FundingPro tomonidan taqdim etilgan**

The FundingPro name/logo must stay visible and link to `https://fundingpro.uz`. Removing it requires a separate white-label agreement.

## Every grant card / page must show

1. A link to the **official source** (`official_url`).
2. The **last verification date** (`last_verified_at`), e.g. “Проверено FundingPro: 16.11.2026”.
3. The disclaimer: *“Always verify eligibility and deadlines with the official announcement.”* / «Условия необходимо сверять с официальным объявлением.» / «Shartlarni rasmiy e’lon bilan solishtiring.»

## Do not

* present FundingPro data as your own;
* change amounts, deadlines or eligibility;
* show grants after a `404` or after the cache window.
